Slice 1 changes Go code and the Makefile compile recipe and leaves a green tree; slice 2 (image, compose smoke) and slice 3 (chart, lint tooling) both depend on slice 1 and can run in parallel with non-overlapping ownership; slice 4 needs everything. Estimates: handwritten production code 150 to 220 Go lines, Makefile and Dockerfile 120 to 180 lines, chart templates, schema and value sets 500 to 700 lines of YAML and JSON, scripts 400 to 600 lines, tests 400 to 550 lines, documentation 150 to 250 lines. The chart and the scripts each exceed the 400-line rescoping signal on their own; they are kept as slices because splitting a chart's templates from its schema, or a verification script from its assertions, would leave no independently reviewable intermediate state. Docker is required for slices 2 and 4; a laptop with about 6 GB free for kind.
